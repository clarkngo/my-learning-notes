The **"Pre-Flight Briefing"** is a fantastic choice because it transforms the AI into a personalized tutor that sets the stage before the student even touches the keyboard. This shifts the students from "clicking through steps" to "looking for specific concepts".

Here is a proposed structure to update your **Technology Tool (TT)** activities using this AI-infused model.

---

## Updated Activity Structure: The Pre-Flight Briefing

To keep this within the **15–30 minute** window, we will split the time between AI interaction, the lab, and the summary.

### Phase 1: AI Prompting (5 Minutes)

Before opening the TryHackMe link, the student must provide a specific prompt to a free AI (Gemini, ChatGPT, etc.).

**The Student Prompt:**

> "I am about to complete a cybersecurity lab on **[Insert Lab Name, e.g., Nmap]**. Based on the typical tasks for this topic, what are the **three most critical concepts** I should look for to understand how this works in a real-world environment? Provide a brief 'cheat sheet' for these three concepts."

### Phase 2: Active Lab Completion (15–20 Minutes)

The student completes the assigned tasks for the room (e.g., Tasks 1-8 for Nmap or Tasks 1-14 for Wireshark ).

* **The Goal:** While doing the lab, they specifically look for the "three critical concepts" the AI identified in Phase 1.

### Phase 3: The Integrated Summary (5–10 Minutes)

The student writes their **150–200 word summary**  with a slight twist to the requirements:

* 
**Understandings:** What did you learn from the lab tasks?


* 
**Findings:** Did the AI’s "Pre-Flight Briefing" accurately predict what was important? 


* 
**Interesting Fact:** What is one "Pro Tip" or "Interesting Fact" the AI gave you that you actually saw in action during the lab?

## Why this works for your students:

* **Zero Cost:** It uses free tiers of existing AI models.
* **Efficiency:** The AI briefing takes less than 5 minutes but provides a "mental map" that makes the 15-minute lab easier to navigate.
* **Validation:** It teaches students to verify AI information against a "source of truth" (the lab results).

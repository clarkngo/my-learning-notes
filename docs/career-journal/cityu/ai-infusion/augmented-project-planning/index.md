To enhance these modules with AI infusion while keeping the changes minimal, you can introduce specific AI-driven tasks that utilize Jira’s existing automation or external generative AI tools to assist with project management artifacts.

Here are suggestions for infusing AI into your ten documents:

### **Foundational AI Strategy (TT01 – TT03)**

Focus on using AI to generate the initial project structure and "dummy" data to save students time and demonstrate automated planning.

* 
**TT01 (Getting Started):** Ask students to use a generative AI tool to brainstorm five common sub-tasks for a client meeting before manually entering them into the Jira timeline.


* 
**TT02 (Project Planning):** Instead of manually creating the office move hierarchy, have students prompt an AI to "Break down an office relocation into 3 Epics, each with 2 Stories and 3 Sub-tasks" to see how AI structures complex projects.


* 
**TT03 (Project Organization):** Use AI to generate "professional personas" for the six dummy users, including their job titles and key responsibilities, to make the workload simulation more realistic.



---

### **AI for Quality and Control (TT04 – TT07)**

Shift the focus to AI as a reviewer or a logic-builder for workflows and risk assessments.

* 
**TT04 (Change Control):** Ask students to consult an AI to suggest two "IF-THEN" logic rules that could prevent scope creep in a Jira workflow.


* 
**TT05 (Quality):** Have students use AI to generate five edge-case test scenarios for a software feature, which they then import into their "Test" issue type.


* 
**TT06 (Estimates):** Use AI to provide a "second opinion" on story point estimates by feeding it the user story and asking for a recommended Fibonacci score.


* 
**TT07 (Risk Management):** Use AI to brainstorm potential project risks for a "local hardware store app" (the scenario from TT09) and categorize them into the Likelihood/Impact matrix.



---

### **Advanced AI Integration (TT08 – TT10)**

Use AI to analyze project data and help with the "Extreme" or "Hybrid" management transitions.

* 
**TT08 (BigPicture):** In the summary, ask students to reflect on how an AI "Predictive Scheduling" feature could improve the Gantt charts they created in BigPicture.


* 
**TT09 (Extreme PM):** Have students use AI to transform a vague client requirement into a properly formatted "As a user, I want..." user story with clear acceptance criteria.


* 
**TT10 (Hybrid Framework):** Ask students to use AI to identify potential "Waterfall" dependencies that might be missed during the rollout of an "Agile" developed feature.

# Ideas 
### AI for Conflict Resolution
* **Research Statement:** This research investigates the efficacy of Artificial Intelligence as a neutral mediator in interpersonal and organizational disputes. It aims to develop algorithmic frameworks capable of analyzing linguistic patterns and emotional sentiment to suggest real-time de-escalation strategies and unbiased resolution options.

### AI-Powered Voice Agents for Conflict Resolution
* **Research Statement:** This project explores the specific application of conversational voice AI in conflict scenarios. It proposes designing voice agents capable of detecting auditory markers of aggression (tone, pitch, volume) to intervene with calming prompts or structured mediation protocols, thereby reducing the cognitive load on human mediators.

### Breaking the First-Thought Trap: Overcoming the Einstellung Effect
* **Research Statement:** This study proposes a human-AI collaborative framework designed to mitigate the "Einstellung Effect" (the brain's tendency to stick to known solutions). It examines how generative AI can be prompted to introduce divergent thinking patterns and alternative problem-solving pathways, effectively disrupting cognitive rigidity during the ideation phase.

### Enhancing Hands-On Skills Through "Vibe Coding"
* **Research Statement:** This research analyzes the pedagogical impact of "Vibe Coding" (an intuition-led, AI-assisted coding workflow) on novice developers. It seeks to determine if abstracting away low-level syntax management via AI tools allows learners to acquire high-level architectural understanding and practical problem-solving skills more rapidly than traditional methods.

### Streamlining Bureaucracy: Leveraging AI to Tame the Paperwork Beast
* **Research Statement:** This project aims to develop a model for administrative optimization using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs). The research focuses on automating the interpretation, categorization, and processing of complex bureaucratic documentation to significantly reduce administrative bottlenecks and human error.

### Using Voice Mode to Build Confidence in Technical Interviews
* **Research Statement:** This study evaluates the effectiveness of AI voice interaction modes as simulated interview partners. It investigates whether verbal rehearsal with an AI agent improves a candidate's technical articulation, verbal fluency, and psychological confidence during high-stakes coding and system design interviews.

### Research Project: Enhancing Professional Discourse

**Title:** Enhancing Professional Discourse: A Multi-Agent Simulation for Real-Time Feedback on Collaborative Contribution

**Abstract**
Effective workplace communication requires more than linguistic fluency; it demands the situational awareness to know when and how to interject in ongoing multi-party dialogues. This research introduces a "Safe-to-Fail" simulated environment where a learner interacts with two autonomous AI agents acting as co-workers. The system utilizes a Real-Time Value Assessment Engine to evaluate the learner's contributions based on relevance, originality, and constructive impact. Furthermore, the system employs a Generative Scaffolding Module to provide "just-in-time" assistance, offering improved phrasing or follow-up inquiries. This study aims to measure the effectiveness of simulated conversational rehearsal in improving the confidence and "signal-to-noise" ratio of junior professionals in collaborative settings.

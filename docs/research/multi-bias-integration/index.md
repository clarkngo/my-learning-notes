## Research Idea: Ensemble Fairness and Multi-Bias AI Integration

### Research Objective
To investigate whether an "Ensemble of Biased Experts" can produce more equitable outcomes than a single attempt at a "neutral" model. The core hypothesis is that since data is inherently biased, we should acknowledge and isolate specific biases to balance them out in a final decision layer.

### Key Concepts
* **Acknowledge-and-Balance:** Moving away from the "impossible" goal of removing all bias from a single dataset.
* **Representative Ensembling:** Developing or utilizing models with known, specific cultural or demographic focuses.
* **Aggregation Layer:** Researching a "Fairness Controller" that synthesizes outputs from various demographic-specific models to reach a balanced consensus.

### Potential Research Questions
1.  How does an ensemble of models with diverse biases compare to a single "debiased" model in terms of accuracy and fairness?
2.  What mathematical frameworks (e.g., weighted averaging, voting systems) best serve to balance these competing biases?
3.  Can this approach help bridge "data deserts" where certain demographics are historically underrepresented?

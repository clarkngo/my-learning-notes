# Teamfighting & Mechanics

---

## General Mechanics
* **Use Portrait Lock:** Do not rely on auto-targeting in chaotic fights. Tap the enemy hero’s portrait (usually next to your skill buttons) to lock your attacks and skills onto the priority target.
* **Stutter Stepping (Kiting):** Never stand still while attacking. Move the joystick immediately after an auto-attack fires to cancel the animation’s back-swing. This keeps you mobile and harder to hit.
* **Watch for "The Big Ults":** Before a fight, identify the one ability that kills you (e.g., Malphite Ult, Zed Ult). If that ability hasn't been used yet, play passively. Once it's used, play aggressively.

## ADC Teamfighting
* **Front-to-Back:** Do not try to run past the enemy tank to hit their carry. Hit the safest target closest to you (usually the tank). Your job is consistent damage, not assassination.
* **Late Arrival:** Enter the fight 2 seconds after the tanks engage. Let the enemy blow their crowd control (CC) and gap-closers on your teammates first.
* **Save Your Dash:** Never use your dash/blink ability for damage unless it guarantees a kill and your safety. Save it strictly to dodge a skill shot or escape an assassin.

## Bruiser Teamfighting
* **Identify Your Role:**
    * **Peeling:** If your ADC is fed, stand on top of them and kill whoever dives them.
    * **Diving:** If the enemy ADC is fed, flank around the side to zone them out of the fight.
* **Weave Attacks:** Between every ability cast, land one auto-attack. This maximizes damage output and triggers key runes (like Conqueror) or item passives (like Sheen).
* **Disrupt, Don't Just Kill:** You don't always need to kill the backline. Just forcing the enemy mage or ADC to run away means they aren't dealing damage to your team.

## Mage Teamfighting
* **Fight in Choke Points:** Mages thrive in the Jungle, not the Lane. Force fights in narrow jungle paths or around Dragon/Baron pits where enemies are clumped together for your AOE (Area of Effect) spells.
* **Cycle Your Cooldowns:** Dump your full combo, then **retreat**. Do not stay in auto-attack range while your spells are on cooldown. Walk back, wait for spells to recharge, then re-enter.
* **Zone Control:** Place your AOE abilities to cut off enemy escape routes or separate their frontline from their backline.

## Assassin Teamfighting
* **The Flank is Mandatory:** Never run straight down the middle of a lane into a teamfight. Approach from the side or behind using a Sweeper (Red Trinket) to ensure you aren't seen.
* **Patience is Key:** Wait for the "CC usage." If the enemy Support still has their stun, you cannot go in. Wait until they use it on your tank, *then* kill the Carry.
* **Get In, Get Out:** Burst your target and immediately disengage using your mobility or Stasis (Golden) enchant. Do not stick around for extended trades.

## Support Teamfighting
* **Tank Supports (Engage):** Your body is a resource. Block skill shots (like hooks or binding shots) intended for your carry. Once you engage, don't run; stay in the fray to apply slows and soak damage.
* **Enchanter Supports (Peel):** Stay *behind* your ADC. Save your "disengage" tool (Polymorph, Knockback, Heal) specifically for when an Assassin jumps on your carry.
* **Exhaust Timing:** Save the Exhaust summoner spell for the enemy's highest burst moment (e.g., when the Assassin ults), not just to slow someone down for a chase.

# Support

---

## Laning Phase
* **The Parallel Rule:** Imagine a rope connecting you and your ADC. Always position **parallel** to them (side-by-side). If you stand behind them, you force them to take 1v2 harassment.
* **Health is a Resource:** If you have full HP, you aren't doing your job. Step up to threaten the enemy or block skill shots (like Blitzcrank hooks or Ashe arrows) for your ADC.
* **Level 2 All-In:** Help your ADC kill the first wave quickly. The moment you hit Level 2, engage immediately while the enemy is still Level 1. This often burns their Flash or scores a kill.
* **Don't Touch the Wave (Usually):** Unless you have a specific support item (Relic Shield) or your ADC is dead/absent, do not auto-attack minions. It messes up their last-hitting rhythm.

## Roaming & Macro
* **The "Crash & Roam":** The safest time to leave lane is **immediately after** you crash your minion wave into the enemy tower. The enemy is stuck farming, giving you a ~45-second window to walk Mid or check the River.
* **Herald Rotation:** The first Rift Herald (spawns at 5:00) is often more valuable than the first Dragon. If your ADC is safe, rotate to the Herald pit to help your Jungler secure it.
* **Sync Recalls:** Always recall when your ADC recalls. If you stay in lane alone, you risk dying or soaking XP that de-syncs your levels. If you roam, ensure you are back before the wave hits your tower.

## Vision Control
* **The 1:25 River Ward:** Place a ward in the river pixel brush (the small isolated bush) around **1:25**. This spots the enemy Jungler fighting the Scuttle Crab and protects you from early ganks.
* **Deep Vision:** If you have "lane priority" (enemies are pushed under their tower), walk into the enemy jungle and ward their Blue/Red buff. Knowing where the enemy Jungler is *not* is just as valuable as knowing where they *are*.
* **Swap to Sweeper:** After your support item quest completes (or after your first recall), swap your yellow ward for the **Sweeper (Red Trinket)**. Use it to clear vision before objectives like Dragon or Baron.

## Teamfighting
* **Identify Your Job:**
    * **Vanguard (Leona, Alistar):** Engage the fight, then decide: stay deep to disrupt, or walk back to peel for your carry?
    * **Warden/Enchanter (Braum, Lulu):** Your eyes should be glued to your ADC. Ignore the enemy frontline; focus entirely on stopping the Assassin diving your backline.
* **Bodyblocking:** As a tank support, physically stand between the enemy carry and your carry. You can block non-piercing skill shots (like Ezreal Q or Jhin Ult) just by standing there.
* **Save CC for "The Threat":** Don't use your stun on the unkillable tank. Save your most important Crowd Control (Polymorph, Bubble, Hook) for the enemy Katarina or Master Yi the moment they engage.

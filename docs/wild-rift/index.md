# Learning & Mindset

## The Mental Game
* **The 40/40/20 Rule:** You will lose 40% of games no matter how well you play, and win 40% even if you play poorly. You only control the remaining **20%**. Focus your mental energy only on those close games where your decisions actually change the outcome.
* **Mute All to Climb:** Typing never wins games; it only wastes time. If you find yourself typing, go to Settings and set Chat to **"Party Only"**. Communicate strictly through pings.
* **The "Two-Loss" Stop:** If you lose two Ranked games in a row, **stop playing Ranked**. Your brain is fatigued and you are likely "tilted," even if you don't feel it. Play ARAM or take a 15-minute break to reset.

## Effective Learning
* **Review Your Deaths:** You don't need to watch the whole replay. Skip to every time you died. Ask one question: *"Did I die because I was surprised, or because I took a bad fight?"*
    * *Surprised?* = Ward better / Check minimap.
    * *Bad fight?* = Learn champion limits / Don't fight outnumbered.
* **Mastery > Diversity:** It is better to play **2 champions** perfectly than 10 champions averagely. climbing requires you to know your damage limits exactly, which only comes from repetition.

# Game Overview

## The Map & Perspective
* **Mirrored Map:** Unlike PC League, your base is *always* in the bottom-left corner of your screen. If you are on the "Red" team, the map is visually flipped.
    * **Baron Lane:** Always indicated by the purple Baron symbol on the ground. It is usually the *solo* lane.
    * **Dragon Lane:** Always indicated by the blue Dragon symbol. This is for the *duo* (ADC/Support).
    * **Check the Arrows:** At the start of the game, glowing arrows on the floor will guide you to the correct lane. Follow them!

## Win Conditions
* **Turrets are Permanent:** Unlike PC, there are no "inhibitors" to respawn. Once you destroy the Tier 3 (Base) turret, Super Minions will spawn in that lane for the *rest of the game*. This puts permanent pressure on the enemy.
* **The "Elder" Flip:** In the late game (18+ minutes), the **Elder Dragon** spawns. It grants an execute that kills enemies below 20% HP instantly. This is the true "Game Over" mechanic. If the enemy gets this, run away and defend until it expires.
* **Tempo is King:** Wild Rift is faster than PC. Recalls are faster, gold is higher, and the map is smaller. Standing still for 10 seconds is a massive loss. Always be moving toward gold or an objective.

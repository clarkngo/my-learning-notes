# Jungle
## Pantheon
- Jungle clear: use empowered 2nd skill to immediately get 3 stacks. 3rd skill cancel it immediately to stack and return to auto-attack
- Preparing to fight: spam 1st until 5 stacks to gank with empowered 2nd skill.
- Strategy: ganks available with level 2 (1st and 2nd skill). focus on ganks rather than clearing camps.

# Support

## Nami

### Common Mistakes Nami Players Make
* **Playing too passively:** A major mistake, even in high Elo (Diamond/Masters), is not poking enough. Nami is designed to trade aggressively.
* **Targeting the wrong enemy:** Many players try to bubble the enemy support (specifically engage supports). Instead, the priority should be **bubbling the enemy ADC** to stop them from following up on an engage or dealing damage.

---

### How to Play Nami
* **Trading & Aggression:** Nami can trade harder than most other enchanter supports. Do not be afraid to walk up and use your **W (Ebb and Flow)** to deal damage and heal yourself simultaneously.
* **Mastering the Bubble (Q):**
    * **Timing:** Learn the landing time of the bubble so you can predict where the enemy will move.
    * **Zoning:** Zone enemies into a corner or wait until they are stuck in the animation of last-hitting a minion to throw the bubble.
    * **Defense:** If caught, you can escape by kiting—buff yourself for speed and throw a bubble to disengage.
* **The "Mind Game" Combo:** Walk up to trade your W on the enemy support. When the enemy ADC steps forward to trade back, throw the bubble at the ADC immediately.

---

### How to Lane Against Nami
* **Wait out the Cooldowns:** The best window to engage is immediately after she uses or misses her bubble.
* **Movement:** Focus on side-stepping. Unless you are in high Elo, most Nami players throw bubbles directly at you rather than predicting your movement.
* **Champion Picks:** Hook champions are generally strong against her.
* **Respect the Ultimate:** As an ADC, do not engage solo once she has her Ultimate. She will chain CC (crowd control), slow, and sustain her partner. Wait for your support to make the play (e.g., land a hook) before going all-in.

### 🌊 Nami Gameplay Cheat Sheet

#### 1. The "Mind Game" Trade (From the Chat)
This is the specific aggressive strategy mentioned to catch opponents off guard.
* **The Setup:** Walk up aggressively.
* **The Action:** Cast **W (Ebb and Flow)** on the enemy *Support*.
* **The Trap:** As the enemy *ADC* steps forward to trade back against you, immediately throw your **Q (Bubble)** at the ADC's predicted location.
* **Why it works:** You bait them into thinking you are focused on the support, making the ADC walk into a vulnerable spot.

#### 2. Standard Trading & Poke
Use this to apply pressure during the laning phase.
* **Self-Buff:** Cast **E (Tidecaller's Blessing)** on yourself.
* **The Slow:** Auto-attack the enemy (this applies the slow from your **E**, making them easier to hit).
* **The Follow-up:** Cast **W** for damage or throw a **Q** now that they are slowed.

#### 3. The "Guaranteed" Bubble
Bubbles are hard to hit on moving targets. Use these windows to increase accuracy:
* **Last Hit Window:** Watch the enemy minion health. When the enemy ADC stops moving to auto-attack a dying minion, throw **Q**.
* **Chain CC:** Wait for your teammates to land a CC (stun/root) first, *then* throw **Q** on top of them.
* **The Ultimate Setup:** Cast **R (Tidal Wave)** first. The knock-up and massive slow make landing the **Q** almost guaranteed immediately after.

#### 4. Disengage / Survival
If you get caught or ganked:
* **Speed Up:** Cast **E** or **W** on yourself (Nami's passive grants Movement Speed when abilities hit allies/self).
* **Zone:** Throw **Q** slightly *in front* of you (in the path of the enemy chasing you) to force them to dodge or get stunned.

# Macro, Phases & Vision

---

## Game Phases (Timers & Goals)
* **Early Game (0:00 – 7:00):** Focus is **Lane Priority & Plates**.
    * **Turret Plates fall at 7:00.** Your goal is to break these for massive gold before they vanish.
    * **First Objectives:** Dragon and Rift Herald spawn at **5:00**. Ideally, you want lane priority (minions pushed up) by 4:30 to rotate.
* **Mid Game (7:00 – 12:00):** Focus is **Rotations & Outer Towers**.
    * Laning phase is over. Mid laners and ADCs should group Mid to siege; Top laners (Baron) go to side lanes to split push.
    * **Do not share farm:** If the ADC is Mid, the Mid laner *must* go to a side lane to farm. Sharing one wave splits the XP and gold, putting both of you behind.
* **Late Game (12:00+):** Focus is **Baron & Elder**.
    * **Baron Nashor spawns at 12:00.** If you kill two enemies, rush Baron immediately to end the game.
    * **Elder Dragon (~19-20m):** The most powerful buff in the game. It executes enemies below 20% HP. **Never** fight 4v5 when Elder is up; give up everything else to contest this.

## Roaming
* **Shove Then Move:** Never leave your lane if minions are under *your* tower. Kill enemy minions first (shove), *then* roam. This forces the enemy to stay and farm or lose gold to follow you.
* **The "River Check":** You don't always have to gank. Shove your wave and walk into the river pixel brush. If you spot the enemy Jungler, you've saved a teammate. If you see nothing, place a ward and return to lane.
* **Cheater Recall:** On the third minion wave (Cannon wave), shove hard and recall immediately. You will return to lane with an item advantage (e.g., a Long Sword) while the enemy is still stuck farming with low HP/Mana.

## Playing with Leads
* **Don't "ARAM" Mid:** If you take the Mid outer turret, stop grouping 5 Mid. It's inefficient. Spread out to take Tier 1 towers in Top and Bot lanes to open up the map.
* **Steal, Don't Just Kill:** If you kill the enemy Jungler, **take their jungle camps**. This gives you +100 gold and them -100 gold, doubling your lead.
* **Bounties are Dangerous:** If you are 5/0, you have a massive gold bounty on your head. Trading 1-for-1 is a *loss* for you because you give the enemy carry huge "shutdown gold." Play safer when fed.

## Vision Control
* **Defensive vs. Offensive:**
    * **Winning?** Ward the *enemy's* jungle (Blue/Red buff bushes) to catch them rotating.
    * **Losing?** Ward *your own* jungle entrances to stop them from diving you.
* **Pixel Brush is King:** The small isolated bush in the river (Pixel Brush) is the most valuable ward spot in the early game. It spots scuttle crabs, river roams, and ganks for both Mid and Side lanes.
* **Sweeper (Red Trinket):** Supports and Junglers should swap to Sweeper immediately after the first recall. Clearing enemy vision is often more valuable than placing your own, especially around Dragon/Baron pits.

# Advanced Macro: Priority & Splitting

---

## Lane Priority ("Prio")
**Definition:** You have "Prio" when your minion wave is pushed fully into the enemy tower. You are free to move, while the enemy is stuck collecting gold under their turret.

* **First Mover Advantage:** If a fight breaks out in the river (e.g., for Scuttle Crab), you can arrive first. The enemy laner must choose: follow you and lose all the gold/XP from the wave, or stay and let their team fight 4v5.
* **The "Turn" Concept:** Think of LoL/Wild Rift like a turn-based game. When you crash a wave, it is your "turn" to do something on the map. When you are clearing a wave under your tower, it is the enemy's "turn."
* **How to Use Prio:**
    1.  **Deep Ward:** Walk into the enemy jungle and ward their Red/Blue buff.
    2.  **Roam:** Walk halfway to another lane to threaten a gank.
    3.  **Reset:** Recall freely without losing minions.

## Split Pushing
**Definition:** One player (usually Baron Lane) pushes a side lane alone to draw enemy pressure away from the main objective (Dragon/Baron).

* **The Golden Rule:** **Only push when your team is applying pressure elsewhere.** If you push while your team is recalling or farming effectively, the entire enemy team will just collapse on you and kill you for free.
* **The "Cross-Map" Play:** Split push the lane **furthest** from the current objective.
    * *Dragon is spawning?* Push Top.
    * *Baron is spawning?* Push Bot.
    * *Why?* If the enemy sends 2 people to stop you Top, your team gets a free Dragon (4v3 advantage).
* **Duelists Only:** You should only split push if you can win a 1v1 against anyone on the enemy team. If the enemy Support can stop your push alone, you should not be splitting. (Best Champs: Fiora, Jax, Camille, Garen, Tryndamere).
* **Eye on the Minimap:** You are the bait. As you push, watch the minimap. If enemies disappear from Mid lane, **run immediately**. You have already done your job by forcing them to move; you don't need to die for it.

# Team Communication
That is a fantastic macro tip. It is one of the most underused communication tools in *Wild Rift*, and doing it consistently can single-handedly win team fights.

Here is a breakdown of why that specific interaction is so high-value and how to maximize it:

### 1. How It Works (The Mechanics)
You are correct—opening the **Scoreboard** (often called the K/D/A screen) allows you to tap on various enemy assets to broadcast their status to your team chat.
* **Ultimates:** Tap the diamond icon next to the enemy champion's portrait.
* **Summoner Spells (Flash, Ignite, etc.):** Tap the spell icons next to the portrait.
* **Boot Enchants (Stasis, Quicksilver, Teleport):** Tap the item icon itself.

**Note:** Unlike PC *League of Legends*, which requires modifier keys (Alt+Click), *Wild Rift* just requires a simple tap on the scoreboard element.

### 2. Why This Wins Games (The Strategy)
This goes beyond just saying "they used it." It creates a **timestamp** in the chat log.

* **Flash Tracking:** If you ping an enemy Flash at **5:00**, your Jungler knows that lane is a free gank until **10:00** (since Flash has a 5-minute cooldown).
* **Objective Fights:** If you ping that the enemy **Amumu** or **Malphite** used their ultimate right before a Dragon spawns, your team knows they can force a fight immediately because the enemy lacks their massive team-fight tool.
* **Item Spikes:** Pinging a **Zhonya’s (Stasis)** or **Guardian Angel** tells your assassins, "Do not waste your full combo on this person, they will survive it."

### 3. Advanced Variation: "Enemy Vision"
While you are on the subject of pinging to notify teammates, the specific **"Enemy Vision"** ping is vital for Junglers.
* If you see an enemy place a ward, use the specific "Enemy Vision" ping (the red eye icon) on that spot.
* This not only warns teammates but **leaves a visible marker on the minimap** that lasts for the duration of the ward, helping your Jungler path around it.

# Spells and Boot Enchants Cooldown Cheatsheet

Here is your cooldown cheat sheet for *Wild Rift*.

### The "Big One"
This is the most important timer to track. If this is down, the enemy is vulnerable to ganks.

| Spell | Cooldown | Mental Math |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flash** | **150s (2m 30s)** | Current Time + **2.5 mins** |

### Combat Summoners
These usually come back up every "wave and a half" of fighting.

| Spell | Cooldown | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ignite** | 90s (1.5m) | Kill pressure / Anti-heal |
| **Barrier** | 90s (1.5m) | Anti-burst shield |
| **Ghost** | 90s (1.5m) | Chasing / Kiting |
| **Exhaust** | 105s (1m 45s) | Anti-Assassin |
| **Heal** | 105s (1m 45s) | Duo lane survival |

### Boot Enchants (Active Items)
You must check the scoreboard to see if they have bought these yet.

| Enchant | Cooldown | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stasis (Zhonya's)** | **120s (2m)** | *Priority Track.* Do not ult them if this is up. |
| **Teleport** | 180s (3m) | They can split push and join fights late. |
| **Quicksilver (QSS)** | 60s (1m) | Removes Crowd Control (CC). |
| **Protobelt** | 60s (1m) | Dash/Engage. |
| **Locket / Veil** | 60s (1m) | Team shielding. |

### ⚠️ The "Lucidity" Variable
If the enemy buys **Ionian Boots of Lucidity** (the white boots), their Summoner Spell cooldowns are reduced by **15%**.
* **Flash** drops to **~127s** (just over 2 mins).
* **Ignite** drops to **~76s**.

**1. Value Temporary Advantages Over Permanent Ones**
In the late game, **prioritizing a temporary buff like Baron over a permanent objective like a Dragon Soul can actually win you the game**. If the enemy team is posturing to take a Dragon Soul, using that time to instead secure the Baron and gain mid-lane priority is often the superior play. **Do not tunnel vision on contesting dragons**; instead, use the pressure from the Baron buff to push towers and aggressively end the game while the enemy is distracted. 

**2. Exploit Empowered Recalls for Positional Superiority**
Securing the Baron buff grants your team empowered recalls, which allow you to return to base and traverse the map much faster than your opponents. This rapid map movement is a massive advantage when juggling defensive positioning and posturing for major objectives like the Elder Dragon, allowing you to turn a coin-flip scenario into a secure victory.

**3. When Behind, Ask: "How Can We Survive?" and "How Can We Win?"**
If a team fight goes terribly wrong and the enemy secures a game-ending buff like the Elder Dragon, you must immediately shift your mindset. First, ask yourself, **"How can we survive?"**. Your immediate goal is to stall and distract the enemy team without committing to a direct fight, buying crucial seconds for your teammates to respawn. Once your team is back on the map, ask, **"How can we win?"** and look for backdoor opportunities or openings to rush the Nexus towers. 

**4. Refuse to Lose and Ignore the Surrender Vote**
Even when a situation looks incredibly dire, your team is almost aced, and the surrender vote pops up on your screen, **refuse to give up and focus on stalling**. Utilize everything at your disposal to stay alive and waste the enemy's time, such as using blast cones to escape or relying on elemental dragon buffs (like the Ocean Soul) to heal through damage. By refusing to lose, you can buy enough time for your strongest teammates to respawn, catch the enemy team making a mistake, and counter-push down the mid lane to destroy the Nexus.

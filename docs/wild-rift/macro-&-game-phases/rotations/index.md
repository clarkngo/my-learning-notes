### The Mid-Game Rotation

The concept of the ADC (and support) rotating to the mid lane after taking the first Dragon Lane tower is a fundamental part of mid-game macro in Wild Rift. This move allows the ADC to farm more safely in a shorter lane while applying pressure to the center of the map.

### Why this rotation is used:

1. **Safety:** Once the enemy tier 1 tower is down, the lane becomes "long." If the ADC continues to push toward the tier 2 tower, they are highly susceptible to ganks.
2. **Map Pressure:** Moving the ADC/Support duo to mid lane allows them to push the wave quickly and rotate to both the Baron and Dragon pits.
3. **Opening the Map:** The mid-laner, often an assassin or a champ with teleport/mobility, moves to a side lane (usually the one where the tower is already down) to "solo farm" and create split-push pressure.

The mid-game rotation is one of the most debated topics in Wild Rift solo queue because it requires coordination between teammates who might not be on the same page.

Here are the most commonly asked questions (and the nuanced answers) regarding the ADC-to-mid switch:

### 1. "What if the mid-laner refuses to leave?"

This is the #1 solo queue headache.

* **The Problem:** Your mid-laner (like a Ziggs or Lux) wants to stay mid to farm or poke, and if you show up, you’re just splitting XP.
* **The Answer:** If they won't budge, **don't force it.** Splitting XP three ways (ADC, Support, Mid) is a recipe for falling behind. Instead, look to take the Solo Lane (Top) tower or follow your Jungler to force an objective like Rift Herald.

### 2. "Do I switch even if we lost our own Dragon Lane tower?"

* **The Answer:** Generally, **no.** If your tower is down first, you usually need to stay in the Dragon Lane to "freeze" the wave near your Tier 2 tower to farm safely and catch up. Rotating mid while your own lane is pushing into your base just loses you more gold and map control.

### 3. "Does the Support always have to come with me?"

* **The Answer:** **Usually, yes, but with a catch.** The ADC needs the Support in mid to provide vision and peel, as mid is open from two sides. However, this is also the time when the Support should start "roaming" more with the Jungler to set up deep vision, returning to mid only to protect the ADC when the wave is being pushed.

### 4. "When is it better to go Top instead of Mid?"

* **The Answer:** If the **Rift Herald** is up and the **Dragon** is not, rotating Top is often superior. Taking the Top tower with your duo lane + Jungler almost guarantees a Herald, which you can then use to "crack" the Mid tower—the most important tower on the map.

### 5. "What should the Mid-laner do once I take over their lane?"

* **The Answer:** They should move to a side lane (usually the Dragon Lane you just left). Ideally, this should be a champion with **escape tools** or **global presence** (like Kassadin, Zed, or Twisted Fate) who can safely farm and then rotate back to fights quickly.

# Laning & Wave Management

---

## General Laning
* **Respect Minion Aggro:** Early game minions do significant damage. Do not trade auto-attacks with the enemy while standing inside their large minion wave.
* **The Level 2 Spike:** The first player to kill the **7th minion** (usually the first melee minion of the second wave) hits Level 2 first. Engage immediately while you have the stat and ability advantage.
* **Crash Before Recall:** Always kill the enemy minions and crash your wave into their tower before recalling. This denies the enemy gold and causes the wave to bounce back toward you.
* **The "Slow Push":** Only last-hit minions at the very last second to build up a massive wave. Use this to dive the enemy under their tower or rotate to an objective while they are stuck clearing.

## Baron Lane
* **Freeze to Deny:** Because the lane is long, **freezing** the wave just outside your tower range forces the enemy to overextend, making them easy targets for ganks.
* **Ward Deep:** Since you are often on an "island," place wards deeper in the river or enemy jungle entrance to spot rotations early.
* **Split Push Pressure:** If you are ahead, shove the wave deep and threaten the Tier 2 tower to draw multiple enemies to you, freeing up your team to take Dragon or Baron.

## Mid Lane
* **Clear & Roam:** Prioritize clearing your wave quickly (shoving). Once the wave is under the enemy tower, you are free to roam to side lanes or help your jungler invade.
* **Hover Your Jungler:** If your jungler is fighting for the Scuttle Crab or invading, move to help them immediately. Mid lane priority often dictates jungle success.
* **Lean to Vision:** Position your champion on the side of the lane where you have placed a ward or where your jungler is located to avoid surprise ganks.

## Duo Lane (ADC & Support)
* **Race for Level 2:** Both the ADC and Support should hit the first wave hard. Reaching Level 2 first allows for a dominant all-in or heavy poke that can win the lane instantly.
* **Synchronized Recalls:** ADC and Support should recall together. Returning to lane alone usually results in getting zoned out or killed.
* **Support Presence:** **Supports** should position parallel to their ADC (not behind them) to exert pressure. If the Support hides, the ADC takes 1v2 harassment.
* **Cannon Wave Recalls:** The best time to recall is on a **Cannon Minion wave**. It takes the enemy tower longer to kill the cannon, giving you more time to walk back to lane without losing gold.

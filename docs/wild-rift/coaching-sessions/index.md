# Coaching on Brand Support

Better **wave control** and **map awareness** significantly enhance a support's effectiveness by influencing macro play, enabling optimal gold and experience distribution, and creating opportunities for favorable trades and objective control.

### Improving Effectiveness through Wave Control

Wave control, along with timing, is a key component of effective support play and overall macro strategy.

1.  **Managing Experience (XP) and Gold:**
    *   Effective wave management ensures the ADC receives crucial resources. If the ADC is returning to the lane, the support should **avoid fully clearing the minion wave** in order to save the minions for the ADC to get the experience.
2.  **Timing Pushes:**
    *   Supports need to be aware of when to push the wave. For instance, if the opposing team leaves the lane and wanders, the support can then shove the wave.
    *   **Watching the wave a lot is helpful for executing macro plays**.
    *   Aggressive wave control, such as pushing a tower first, can be used to leverage team momentum, forcing the enemy to defend or contest while the team collapses toward an objective.
3.  **Creating Poke Opportunities:**
    *   Wave positioning can set up opportunities for damage. For example, when playing a champion like Brand, the support can stand in a bush and poke while the wave is present.
    *   A critical timing opportunity arises when the enemy support is using a specific support item (like Relic Shield) that requires them to walk up and kill minions; this moment is a **perfect opportunity to use abilities and land poke**.

### Improving Effectiveness through Map Awareness

Map awareness, including **warding and vision control**, is a fundamental responsibility for supports and directly translates into better effectiveness by tracking the enemy and enabling coordinated team movements.

1.  **Vision Control and Warding:**
    *   Vision control is explicitly noted as something that supports should be doing.
    *   Supports should use **control wards (pink wards)**, which are useful to place before objectives.
    *   Warding is essential for setting up ganks; laners, including the support, should ensure a ward is placed in the bush.
2.  **Tracking Enemy Movements (Macro Play):**
    *   Watching the map is generally helpful for macro strategy.
    *   It is **really important to track where the enemy jungler is likely to be** ("ping"). This tracking provides an idea of the enemy jungler's position, which allows the team to make counter plays.
    *   If the support knows the enemy jungler's location, they can ping their teammates to warn them to back up, thereby avoiding a gank.
    *   If the opposing support (or any enemy) is "wandering" (roaming), knowing this detail is critical to adjust play.
3.  **Lane Gank Setup:**
    *   Supports can increase the effectiveness of ganks by coordinating wave state with the jungler. Laners, including the support, should **avoid shoving the minion wave all the way to the enemy tower** if the jungler is waiting for a gank, as this makes it harder for the jungler to reach the enemy.

Effectively using both wave control and map awareness allows the support to transition from being reactive to being proactive, much like a traffic conductor who not only sees where the cars are but also strategically manages the flow to ensure everyone reaches their destination safely and efficiently.

Based on the coaching session, there are several additional specific points regarding playing **Brand support**, focusing on champion capabilities, lane strategy, survival, and general mastery.

### I. Brand-Specific Lane Strategy and Poking

1.  **Strategic Poking from Bushes:** You can stand in the bushes and **poke** when the minion wave is present. However, be careful when doing this, as enemies like Ezreal can check the bushes.
2.  **Exploiting Enemy Support Items:** A critical time to use abilities and land poke is when the enemy support is walking up to execute a minion to proc a specific support item (like Relic Shield). This makes for a **perfect opportunity to use abilities and land poke**.
3.  **Purposeful Ability Usage (Mana Management):** Mana management is important for Brand, especially since playing from behind is hard.
    *   **Don't use abilities just for the sake of it**. Ensure there is a reason for your poke.
    *   Make sure you are going in for a worthwhile trade, such as when the enemy has used their abilities or is out of position.
    *   A general guideline is to ensure you do not reach zero mana and always retain enough mana for an **emergency poke or to escape**.

### II. Matchups, Positioning, and Survival

1.  **Learning Matchups:** It is crucial to learn champion matchups better. Brand is currently considered a low-tier (C-tier) champion.
2.  **Playing Against Engage Tanks:** You must be extremely careful when facing tank-heavy compositions or engage supports like **Nautilus** and **Rakan**, as Brand is squishy.
    *   Nautilus can easily catch and root you (can "free free and just you").
    *   Rakan is an engage champion who can dash, knock up, and then go out again. Try to poke out Rakan and mess up her tempo before she reaches level five.
3.  **Safety and Positioning:** Brand lacks strong disengage abilities (like dashes or jumps) that some teammates (Caitlyn, Jax, Irelia) might have. You need to be careful not to get caught out.
    *   Avoid consistently poking from the same spot, as opponents will predict where you are.
    *   If you are getting caught often, consider building an item like **Zhonya's Hourglass (Stasis)** earlier (second or third item) to buy time for teammates to follow up.
    *   Against teams with heavy shields, consider building **Serpent's Fang** to counter the shield strength.
4.  **Dying:** You were observed to be "dying a lot" and were advised to focus on play style improvements to reduce deaths.

### III. General Champion Mastery

1.  **Focusing Your Champion Pool (Micro Play):** A core recommendation is to **stick to one or two champions at most** to get a feel for their play style and abilities.
    *   Improving your **micro** play (champion mechanics, abilities, capabilities, and positioning, e.g., staying out of range) is necessary before advancing further.
    *   Getting comfortable with a champion allows the execution of abilities and optimal clearing to become ingrained, functioning as **muscle memory**.
2.  **Utilizing Teammate Communication:** You should take on more responsibility for macro decisions and ask questions to your duo partner, such as "why are we doing this?" or "why are we staying?" when discussing pushing and lane management.

***

**Analogy for Champion Mastery:**

Think of mastering Brand like learning to play a musical instrument: you must first dedicate yourself to mastering the basic scales, chords, and techniques (your micro and Brand's specific capabilities/combos) until they become muscle memory. Only once those fundamentals are automatic can you effectively focus on composing complex music (macro strategy and reading the enemy team) without fumbling the basics.

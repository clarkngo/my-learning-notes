# Recalling

Source: talanwr's [This Wild Rift skill is way more important than you think](https://youtu.be/ixYdazXV4ao)

### I. General Recalling Principles

1.  **Always Push the Wave First:** Before recalling, you need to **push the wave** to prevent losing minions that would otherwise die to your own minions. This is a crucial step for nearly all recalls.
2.  **Recall on Item Spikes:** Generally, aim to recall when you have enough gold to complete a good item or an **item spike**. If you only have a small amount of gold (e.g., 500 gold in a late-game context), you should often farm more instead of recalling immediately.
3.  **Post-Play/Fight Recall:** After a play works out, such as winning a fight, getting a kill, or securing an objective (Baron, Dragon), make sure to **take everything you can from that side of the map** (turret plates, camps, waves) **before resetting**.
4.  **Recall Before Objectives:** A key mid-to-late game tip is to reset **20 to 45 seconds before an objective spawns** if you plan on contesting it. This ensures you are prepared and back on the map in time.

### II. Early Game Recalls

#### A. First Recall (Laners: Mid, ADC, Top)
1.  **Boots Timer:** Your first recall typically should be scheduled around the time you acquire enough gold for **boots**. You should find your recall as close to boots as possible.
2.  **Timing Flexibility:** While you want to recall on boots, the exact timing might be a wave earlier or a wave later, depending on when you find an opportunity to **crash the wave** under the enemy tower.
3.  **Staying Longer:** You can sometimes stay longer (a wave later) even after getting boots if the wave is still present and your jungler is on your side of the map, giving you relative safety. Staying longer allows you to continue to press a lead and gain more gold.

#### B. Early/Emergency Recalls
1.  **Low Health/Mana:** If you are too low on health or mana to safely stay, you can recall a wave or two earlier than the boots timer. Mages often do this due to lower mana early on.
2.  **Emergency Item Purchase:** If you recall early, buy whatever item component will give you some level of damage. For example, buy a Lost Chapter or Pendant for a mage, or a Serrated Dirk for an AD champion. **Do not buy brown boots** if you recall early due to low resources.

#### C. Dual Lane Recalls (ADC and Support)
1.  **Recalling Together:** The Support and ADC should try their best to **reset together**.
2.  **Recall on ADC's Boots:** The recall should ideally happen on the **ADC's boots timer**.
3.  **Support Timing:** Supports often recall before they get full boots because they have less gold income, but they must still reset with their ADC.

#### D. Jungler First Recalls
1.  **Full Clear + Gank:** Recall after completing your **full clear** and successfully pulling off a **gank**, securing all possible advantages like turret plates, before returning to farm.
2.  **Scuttle Clear:** The alternative is to recall simply after clearing the **scuttle**, especially if you determined there was no viable gank opportunity.

### III. Mid to Late Game Recalls

1.  **Second Recall (Laners):** This typically occurs after your boots recall, following either a play or the act of **crashing a big wave** under the turret. If a dive is possible after the wave crash, dive, otherwise, simply recall.
2.  **Second Recall (Junglers):** This usually happens after your second full clear or your second successful gank.
3.  **Team Coordination:** If your teammates are resetting, you often want to **reset together** if you already have your necessary gold or item spike.
4.  **Farm During Team Back-offs:** If your teammates are overextended, dead, or retreating, you may want to farm a bit more before resetting, even if you have your item spike, because you won't be able to make a play with them immediately.
5.  **Baron Recall Timing:** It is critical to reset for Baron anywhere from **30 to 50 seconds (or 1 minute) before it spawns**. Before doing this, **push out a wave on the opposite side of the map**.

Effective recalling can be compared to catching a specialized commuter train: You don't just jump on the train (recall) whenever you feel like it. You need to ensure you've loaded all your luggage (pushed the wave and secured gold), and you need to time your trip carefully so you arrive at the crucial meeting (the objective or item spike) right when it starts, not too early and definitely not too late.


# How to Counter (Classes & Mechanics)

---

## Countering Tanks (Mundo, Malphite, Ornn)
* **Ignore or Shred:** Either kill them *last* (dive the backline) or build specifically to kill them *first*. Do not half-commit.
* **The "Giant Slayer" Rule:** If the enemy has 2+ tanks, ADCs and Mages *must* select the **Giant Slayer** rune in the pre-game lobby. It grants bonus damage based on bonus HP.
* **Itemize % Penetration:** Flat penetration (like Youmuu’s) is useless here. You need **% Armor/Magic Penetration** (Mortal Reminder, Void Staff, Terminus) and **% HP Damage** (Blade of the Ruined King, Liandry’s, Divine Sunderer).
* **Don't Group Tight:** Against engage tanks like Malphite or Amumu, stay scattered. If your team is spread out, their ultimate hits 1 person instead of 5.

## Countering Assassins (Zed, Akali, Master Yi)
* **Exhaust is King:** The Support (or Mid) should take the **Exhaust** summoner spell. Use it the moment the Assassin commits to a fight to reduce their damage by 40%.
* **Stasis (Zhonya's):** If you are the target, buy the Stasis boot enchant early. Use it to dodge their primary burst (e.g., Zed Death Mark pop, Fizz Shark).
* **Crowd Control (CC):** Assassins are squishy. Save your hard CC (Stun, Root, Knockup) exclusively for them. Do not waste it on the Tank.
* **Vision Control:** Assassins love to flank. Place wards over walls and in the river to spot them before they get behind you.

## Countering Mages (Lux, Vex, Brand)
* **Dodge Perpendicular:** Most mage spells are linear skill shots. Dodge *sideways* (90 degrees), not backwards.
* **Buy Magic Resist:** Even a small amount helps. **Wit’s End** (for ADCs) or **Force of Nature** (for Tanks) drastically reduces their burst potential.
* **Bait the Cooldowns:** Pretend to walk into their range, then step back immediately to bait out their main spell (e.g., Lux Bind). Once they miss, you have ~8 seconds to punish them freely.

## Countering Enchanters (Yuumi, Soraka, Lulu)
* **Anti-Heal/Shield is Mandatory:**
    * **Vs Healers:** Buy **Executioner’s Calling** (AD) or **Oblivion Orb** (AP) immediately—do not wait to finish the full item.
    * **Vs Shielders:** AD Assassins/Bruisers should build **Serpent’s Fang**; Mages should build **Oceanid’s Trident**. These items melt shields instantly.
* **Focus Them First:** In teamfights, Enchanters are often the highest priority target. If you kill the Soraka, the unkillable tank in front of her suddenly becomes killable.

## Countering Split Pushers (Fiora, Jax, Hullbreaker users)
* **Force the 5v4:** If the enemy Fiora is splitting Top and her team is Mid, **engage on the 4 enemies Mid immediately**. You must win the fight and take an objective before Fiora takes your inhibitor.
* **Collapse Safely:** If you go to stop the split pusher, send **at least 2 people** (one with CC). Never send a squishy carry alone; they will just die and feed the splitter more.
* **Clear the Wave:** Do not chase the champion; kill their minion wave first. Without minions, they cannot take your tower.

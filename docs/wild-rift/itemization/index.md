### **Wild Rift Itemization Strategy: The "Anti-Heal Trap"**

**The Rule:**
**Do not rush Executioner’s Calling (or Oblivion Orb) as your first item**, even when facing heavy healers like Soraka, Sona, or Yuumi.

**The Logic (Why It Fails Early):**
1.  **The Math of "Grievous Wounds":** Anti-heal reduces healing by a percentage (usually 40%). In the early game (levels 1–5), enemy heals are naturally weak due to low ability power and cooldowns.
    * *Example:* If a Sona heals for 80 HP, anti-heal only blocks **32 HP**.
    * *The Alternative:* If you spent that gold on raw Attack Damage (AD) instead, your attacks would likely deal **50+ extra damage** per combo, effectively "out-damaging" the heal without needing the specific counter-item.
2.  **Opportunity Cost (The 900g Deficit):**
    * Executioner's Calling costs roughly **900 gold**.
    * Buying this early delays your **First Core Item** (like Youmuu's Ghostblade or Infinity Edge) by several minutes.
    * If the enemy ADC ignores anti-heal and finishes their Core Item first, they will stat-check you and win the fight, regardless of how much you reduce their support's healing.

**When TO Buy Anti-Heal:**
Wait until you have completed your **first full item**.
* By the time you finish your first item (around the first Dragon fight), enemy healing will have scaled up enough that the 40% reduction becomes mathematically valuable.

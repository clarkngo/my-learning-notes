# Jungle Timers
This section is extracted from [talanwr](https://www.youtube.com/@talanwr)'s YouTube video: [A rank 1 Player Explains Jungle Timers
](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1GMb8FeyQo) 

## Camp Respawn Timers
The respawn rates of jungle camps dictate the ideal timing for returning to each side of the map.
• Buff Camps (Blue/Red): These camps take 2 minutes and 30 seconds to respawn.
• Small Camps (Gromp, Krugs, Wolves, Raptors): These camps take 1 minute 50 seconds to respawn.
By clearing the buffs first, they will respawn closer to when the small camps spawn, which helps make the next clear round faster and reduces the time spent on that side of the map.

## Efficient Clear and Rotation Timers
The following rough timers outline the typical cadence for a jungler who executes a full clear (clearing all six starting camps) at the beginning of the game:
1. Return to Original Side: The jungler should aim to recall and be back on the side they started on (e.g., Blue side) around 2:25 or 2:30. Small camps typically start respawning around 2:30. If Blue was cleared first, the Gromp might spawn closer to 2:40.
2. Switch Sides: After clearing the first side, the jungler should go back to the other side (e.g., Red side) around 3:45. The small camps on the red side will roughly be up by this time.
3. Final Return to Original Side: The jungler should go back to the original side around 5 minutes.
These are considered rough timers, and they change if you are inefficient for any reason.
## Gold Loss and Inefficiency
Failing to clear camps when they are up results in significant lost gold, delaying power spikes.
• A camp gives roughly 150 gold (depending on the point of the game).
• If a camp is not killed, you are losing over a gold per second.
• If three camps are alive, the jungler is losing roughly three gold per second.
• For example, if you miss three camps for a minute, you lose 450 gold.
## Windows for Action
When being efficient, there are optimal "windows" to look for ganks or other actions.
• After clearing the second set of camps (around 3:00 to 3:10), the jungler has a window until roughly 3:45 before they need to rotate to the next side of the map. During this period, you have about 35 seconds to gank, recall, ward, or sweep.
• You should not look for ganks or fights when camps are available to clear, as this leads to inefficiency and loss of gold.
## Objective Timers
Timers are also critical for objective control.
• To prepare for major objectives (like dragon), the jungler should aim to recall around 5 minutes 30 seconds.
• The general rule is to recall 45 to 30 seconds before an objective spawns to spend accumulated gold, get vision setup (like a sweeper), and prepare with teammates.
• As objectives spawn, efficiency becomes secondary, and prioritization of the objective fight (especially around Baron) is more important than farming every single camp.
The concept of jungle timers acts like a precise scheduler for a delivery driver: they tell you exactly when the next packages (camps) are ready to be picked up across town to ensure you maximize your revenue (gold) before switching your focus to special, higher-value deliveries (objectives)

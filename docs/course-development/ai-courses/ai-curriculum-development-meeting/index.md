## **Meeting Summary: AI Curriculum Development**

Based on the audio transcript and the strategic plan discussed with Sam, the distinction between **Core** and **Depth of Study** courses is the primary framework for the new curriculum design.

Here is what needs to be done with each:

### **1. Core Courses: The "No-Touch" Foundation**

These courses serve as the universal baseline for all technology students. Sam’s current directive is to **leave these as they are** for the time being.

* **Stability:** Core courses like **IS 201** (Fundamentals of Computing), **CS 132** (Computer Science I), and **CS 231** are considered sufficient foundation for now.
* **Function:** They provide the "prep" knowledge required for students to eventually tackle more advanced graduate-level work.

### **2. Depth of Study: The Specialized 6-Course Sequence**

The "Depth of Study" is where the actual differentiation of the new **BS in Artificial Intelligence** occurs. You are tasked with selecting and designing a specific **6-course sequence** for this section.

* **The "50/50" Rule:** Sam requires that exactly **50% (3 courses)** of this depth sequence be reused from existing **Data Science (DS)** or **Computer Science (CS)** catalogs to reduce operational costs.
* **The New 50%:** The remaining **3 courses** must be new, specialized AI courses, such as **AI Agent (AI 240)** or **Agentic AI (AI 250)**.
* **Integration:** These courses must transition from standard "Software Engineering" (writing code) toward **"System Architecture"** (designing AI-driven systems).

# AI Orchestration: From Builder to Conductor

This category documents the fundamental shift in my workflow. I no longer "write code" in the traditional sense; I orchestrate a workforce of specialized agents. My value has moved from **syntax execution** to **architectural intent.**

### Core Philosophy
- **Declarative Intent:** I describe the *what*, and the agents determine the *how*.
- **Resource Management:** Tokens are the new currency of development.
- **Context as Code:** High-fidelity notes are the primary "source of truth" for the AI.

**The Theory:** *The Abstraction Ladder.* In 2026, we have reached the highest rung: **Natural Language Abstraction**. My role is now to maintain the structural integrity of the system while the AI handles the implementation details.
